The goal of the recommendation description is to add a little context on the product sold. As usual keep it simple. Get the info from the technical sheets provided.

Example:

This is [Product] is made by [Manufacturer]. It is a [Type of product] [Product Type i.e. Glove, Helmet], its recommended to be used for [Area of use] and/or [Area of use]. Is it certified to the following [Norms and certifications].
